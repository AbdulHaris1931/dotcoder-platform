# DotCoder 2.0 — UX Research & Competitive Benchmarking Report

> **Document Purpose:** Systematic UX research, persona modeling, cognitive load analysis, and competitive product benchmarking for the DotCoder 2.0 institutional EdTech SaaS platform.  
> **Deployment Site:** Annai Vailankanni Arts and Science College, Thanjavur (`AVASC-TNJ`)  
> **Date:** November 2026

---

## 1. Primary User Groups & Architectural Role Verification

### 1.1 The Student Persona
* **Target Audience:** Final-year and pre-final-year undergraduate and postgraduate students (BCA, B.Sc CS, B.Sc IT, MCA).
* **Primary Motivations:**
  * Clearing Tier-1 and Tier-2 corporate technical assessments (Zoho, TCS NQT, Cognizant, Infosys).
  * Practicing curriculum topics without getting lost in endless uncurated problem banks.
  * Receiving instant execution feedback without needing local Python/C++ compiler setup.
  * Building a verifiable ATS placement resume with authentic credentials.
* **Key Pain Points:**
  * Complex, unguided problem statements leading to cognitive overload.
  * Fear of anti-cheating proctoring false-positives (accidental window defocus, eye tracking bugs).
  * Disconnect between daily practice and actual company recruitment assessment patterns.

### 1.2 The Placement Officer (TPO) / Admin Persona
* **Target Audience:** Head of Training & Placement, Placement Coordinators, Department Placement Faculty.
* **Primary Motivations:**
  * Achieving high placement conversion percentages across all eligible departments.
  * Real-time visibility into student readiness before corporate recruiters arrive on campus.
  * Seamless candidate shortlisting based on minimum cutoff criteria.
  * 1-click export of compliant placement reports for institutional audits (NAAC Criterion 5.2.1, NIRF).
* **Key Pain Points:**
  * Tracking student readiness across hundreds of manual Google Forms and Excel spreadsheets.
  * High recurring compute bills when hosting online coding exams for 1,000+ candidates simultaneously.
  * Inability to verify whether student submissions are authentic or generated by external AI assistants.

### 1.3 The Tutor / Faculty Role — Architectural Verification
* **Investigation:** The codebase, Supabase database schema (`supabase/schema.sql`, `supabase/migrations/`), GoTrue Auth profiles, and TypeScript definitions (`src/types/index.ts`) were systematically audited for the existence of a `tutor` role.
* **Definitive Finding:** The backend strictly supports four role strings:
  ```ts
  export type UserRole = "student" | "admin" | "super_admin" | "tpo";
  ```
  There is **zero existing schema, table, migration, or authorization rule** for a distinct Tutor role.
* **Design Decision:** In accordance with the Project Constitution (AGENTS.md) and User Guidelines (*"Do not fabricate missing backend functionality or introduce new roles as part of a UI-only transformation"*), we do not fabricate non-existent Tutor backend endpoints. Faculty mentors utilize the TPO/Admin view with department-level filtering to review assigned cohorts.

## 2. Persona Profiles & Behavioral Archetypes

### Persona A: Karthik S. (Final-Year BCA Student)
* **Demographics:** Age 21, Final-Year BCA, Annai Vailankanni Arts and Science College, Thanjavur.
* **Goal:** Secure a day-1 placement offer from Zoho Corporation or TCS NQT.
* **Environment:** Practice on a low-to-mid-range laptop running Google Chrome; smartphone used for dual-camera proctoring.
* **Behavior:** Highly motivated by visual progress indicators (streaks, heatmap, readiness score out of 100). Frustrated when coding environments are slow to compile or have rigid whitespace evaluation.
* **Key User Task:** Solve 2 problems daily from the Coursera-style curriculum, complete weekend corporate mock rounds, and export verified ATS placement resume.

### Persona B: Prof. R. Anandhan (Head of Training & Placement)
* **Demographics:** Age 46, Head of Placement Cell, AVASC Thanjavur.
* **Goal:** Place 85%+ of the eligible 2026 graduating batch across BCA, B.Sc CS, B.Sc IT, and MCA.
* **Environment:** Desktop workstation running Windows 11 / Chrome, reviewing student progress reports and recruiter spreadsheets.
* **Behavior:** Needs high-level institutional KPI strips (Total Students, Placement Ready, Active Learners) combined with granular candidate inspection drawers to intervene with at-risk students.
* **Key User Task:** Monitor cohort readiness telemetry, create custom proctored assessment rounds, advance candidate stages in recruitment drives, and export NAAC Criterion 5.2.1 CSV reports.

## 3. Cognitive Load Analysis & Task Walkthroughs

### 3.1 Cognitive Load Evaluation in Coding Workspace (DotLab)
* **Intrinsic Load:** The cognitive effort required to understand the algorithmic problem (e.g. Hash Map complementary search in Two Sum).
* **Extraneous Load (Target of Optimization):** The mental effort spent navigating the interface, configuring compiler flags, switching tabs, or finding test case inputs.
* **DotCoder 2.0 Solution:**
  * **3-Pane Split Layout:** Keeps Problem Specification, Monaco Editor, and Test Result Dock visible simultaneously without context switching.
  * **6-Pill Diagnostic Dock:** Summarizes test execution at a glance (`TC 1` to `TC 6`), showing pass/fail status and execution time in under 50ms.
  * **Progressive Hint Disclosure:** 3-tier accordion hints (Intuition → Data Structure → Edge Case Trap) prevent premature solution spoiling while unblocking stuck learners.

### 3.2 Cognitive Load in Assessment & Proctored Exam Room
* **Exam Anxiety Mitigation:** Clear, persistent countdown timer (`MM:SS`) with warning state only in the final 5 minutes.
* **Question Palette:** Unified grid showing Question Category (MCQ vs Code), Status (Answered, Unanswered, Marked for Review), reducing fear of missing unattempted questions.

## 5. Design Takeaways for DotCoder 2.0

1. **Strategic Accent Hierarchy:** Never use Electric Lime (`#9DFF00`) for large backgrounds or decorative surfaces. Reserve it strictly for primary actions, active navigation pills, and verified credential highlights (70% neutral, 20% dark, 10% lime).
2. **Dedicated Dark Developer Canvas:** DotLab IDE must remain an immersive dark workspace (`#0B0F17`) optimized for hours of sustained code editing, contrasting against the crisp, clean dashboard surfaces.
3. **Data Density with Breathing Room:** Tables and KPI cards must present high-density institutional data using tabular numerals (`font-mono`) without visual crowding.
